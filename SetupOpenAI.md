## Student guide (get your project API key)

1. **Accept the invite**

* Open the email invite to join the instructor’s **Organization** and accept it.

2. **Open your assigned project**

* Log into the OpenAI Platform
* Switch to your instructor’s **Organization**
* Open the project named for you (e.g., `firstname-lastname`)

3. **Create your API key**

* In your project: **API Keys** → **Create new secret key**
* Copy it **once** and store it securely on your computer.

4. **Use it in your dev environment (don’t paste into code)**

* Never commit keys to GitHub.
* The first cell in each lecture/lab notebook will prompt you to paste in this key.

5. **If your key is exposed**

* Tell the instructor immediately.
* Create a new key and stop using the old one (the instructor may remove your access or rotate keys).
